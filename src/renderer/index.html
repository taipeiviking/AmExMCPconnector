Accounts and portfolios

Choose the source every time.

Account

One separately authorized U.S. connection or one Japan import-only source. Its country, personal/company class, legal entity, currency, and user label stay attached.

Portfolio

An owner-controlled local selection of accounts for reporting. It is a view, not a merged ledger, and never changes account authorization.

  • Every ChatGPT call names one account or one portfolio.
  • No hidden default account and no global account enumeration.
  • Personal and company accounts can share a portfolio without losing source attribution.

ChatGPT and offboarding

You remain in control.

ChatGPT can

List selected accounts/status, read scoped transactions/statements, produce a portfolio report, and list/explain review-only alerts.

ChatGPT cannot

Pay, transfer, control a card, dispute, authorize, sync, import, export, post to accounting, change labels, or delete data.

Stop access: Lock the app or exit it; the local MCP endpoint stops.

Revoke or disconnect: A future U.S. provider must be revoked through its official consent controls. Japan import-only accounts have no Amex credential to revoke.

Delete: Settings → Delete local financial data inventories and removes only the encrypted database and app-marked folders after two confirmations. Onboarding preferences, external consent, tunnel profiles, and copies elsewhere remain.
